<objective>
REPR-06: make **both** overflow regimes detectable, each with its own control.

Operator overflow is **LOUD** (`a*a` at `a=1e299` → `UNDF`, `*** Error … overflow in *
operation (mulop)`, rc=3). Intrinsic overflow is **SILENT** (`power(10,300)`,
`power(10,400)`, `exp(1000)` all clamp to exactly `1.0000E+299` at rc=0, `Normal
completion`) — one hundred orders of magnitude collapsed with no diagnostic.

Guards therefore key on **magnitude** against a single named `SATURATION_SENTINEL`,
never on `= INF` — neither regime produces INF, so an `= INF` guard tests false and
execution simply continues.

Purpose: the silent regime is the single most dangerous failure mode this repo has, and
until now the only detector for it was banned outright.
Output: the sentinel and its pin, `make check-saturation`, two lint rules, and four
committed controls.
</objective>

<binding_facts>
1. **Two regimes, and they differ.** Operator overflow: `a*a` at `a=1e299` yields `UNDF`
   with `*** Error … overflow in * operation (mulop)` at **rc=3**. Intrinsic overflow:
   `power(10,300)`, `power(10,400)` and `exp(1000)` all clamp to **exactly `1.0000E+299`**
   at **rc=0** with `Normal completion`.
2. **`= INF` is not a usable non-finiteness guard.** Neither regime produces INF.
3. **N3 — the tautology this plan exists to avoid.** Roadmap rev 4 wrote the guard as
   `abort$(SATURATION_SENTINEL <> exp(1000))`. Both sides recompute the same expression:
   measured rc=0, **it can never fire**. That is the identical defect as the
   `diStarPlankReal` bridge the roadmap catalogues — written into a new criterion by the
   same author who catalogued it. The working pattern is a **committed pin**: the pin
   holds the MEASURED value, the build-time `exp(1000)` is asserted **against the pin**,
   and a mutant that edits the pin reddens.
4. **Exactly one named constant is permitted.** The blanket ban on a saturation constant
   forbade the only working detector for the silent regime; the permitted form is a
   single `SATURATION_SENTINEL`, declared once in the scales module, re-derived at build
   time so it cannot go stale across GAMS versions, and checked against the pin so the
   check can actually fail.
5. **Any new gating recipe must fall inside `lint-make`'s enumeration.** `lint-make`
   pulls recipe bodies from `make --print-data-base`, so `mk/saturation.mk` is reviewed
   automatically: every compound recipe line must begin `set -e;` (LM-SET-E) and no
   grep-derived predicate may appear in a gating position (LM-GREP-PREDICATE).
   `lint-make` will go from 13 recipes to 14 — that is expected, and the finding count
   must stay 0.
6. Registry rows driven through `make` use `expect = nonzero`; rows invoking `gams`
   directly pin the exact code.
