<objective>
REPR-03 + REPR-05: give the four tick concepts four names, make the minimum tick
negative, and make **Δᵢ = 200 expressible**.

Measured today: `primitives.gms` declares `maxTick /16777215/` (2²⁴−1 — the *unsigned*
24-bit max, a fifth concept nobody named) and `minTick /8388607/` — a **positive
minimum** — while `PricingKernel.gms` declares `MAX_TICK /8388607/`,
`MIN_TICK /-8388607/`. Neither matches Uniswap's usable `±887272`, and `-8388607` is not
the two's-complement int24 floor `-8388608`. Four distinct concepts are colliding in
five wrong constants.

And `PricingKernel.gms` declares `Set tickSpacingDomain /s1*s60/`, which caps Δᵢ at 60 —
so `tunablePricingKernel` **cannot express Δᵢ = 200**, which is where SOLVE-04's
`riskNeutral_corner` optimum sits. That corner is currently unreachable by construction.

Purpose: PROG-05 (Phase 8) fails any solution outside these bounds, and VPATH-06
(Phase 10) decodes `int24 tick` / `uint160 sqrtPrice` against them. A wrong bound here
propagates as a wrong *refusal* later.
Output: the four named concepts, the two-level spacing domain with data-driven values,
Δᵢ=200 reachable, and three committed mutants.
</objective>
